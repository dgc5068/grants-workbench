## Idaho Discovery Week

This summer my wife Moema and I started a summer learning camp called
Idaho Discovery Week. We hosted three classes with Moema taking intro to drawing
and intro to Japanese, while I taught electronics and circuitry. In total, over 100 students signed up for our classes!

## What we learned in class

I built the class around the Arduino Nano. We started with the boring
part first, basic physics: voltage, current, resistance, what a circuit
even is. After that every new component got introduced through a
project. Here's some of my favorites:

- **Digital piano** — a 4x4 matrix keypad and a passive buzzer. Each
  button plays a note.
- **Dinosaur game** — a recreation of the one from Google Chrome,
  played with real buttons.
- **Stoplight circuit** — I thought this way a good way to teach logic gates.
- **Star Wars spaceship game** — the final project. Everyone built one
  and got to take it home.

## What I got out of it

People always say the best way to learn something is to teach it. I've
heard that for years but I understand it a lot better now. When a 14-year-old asks
you why the led is backwards, "because it does" isn't an answer.
You have to really know. Three months of that and I understand
microcontrollers way better than I did going in.
